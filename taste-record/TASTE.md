# TASTE.md

What looks chosen and what looks defaulted, for one person, across every
project they build. Agents read this before any design work. Only the owner
adds to it; agents propose in `observations.md`.

## Principles

- Taste reads as removal. Most of what a generator adds to look designed is
  what the owner takes away.
- One deliberate signature per screen, not one per element.

## Hard rejections

Each is "never X; instead Y", with the date it was declared and where it is
enforced. "Not yet enforced" means agents are told, and nothing checks.

- **Never a coloured band along the top of a card; instead, no band.**
  Declared 2026-10-17. Not yet enforced.
- **Never a title in a colour; instead, titles in the normal text colour,
  and colour on a chip or an icon.** Declared 2026-10-24. Not yet enforced.
- **Never a filled button for a suggestion; instead, an outline.** A filled
  control means the person's own answer. Declared 2026-10-24. Not yet
  enforced.
- **Never a label that starts lower case; instead, sentence case.**
  Declared 2026-10-24. Enforced by a test over the copy file.
- **Never pure black on pure white; instead, a tinted pair.** Declared
  2026-07-26. Enforced by the colour tokens.

## Thresholds

- Body text no smaller than 16 pixels; whole-pixel sizes only.
